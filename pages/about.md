## Susan Mills, Founder & CEO

Susan Mills has been a business companion to information technology entrepreneurs and community initiatives for over four decades. Through shifting world and technology contexts, she has helped more than 200 companies and initiatives enter markets and achieve operating impact. Many of her multi-year engagements carried a client from the pre-funded concept stage through IPO and market leadership.

Today the stakes are much higher than competitive dominance. Success will be defined in deeper and broader terms that build humanity's collective thriving. The rewards will go to decision-makers who bring business, personal, world, environmental and spiritual perspectives together to create lasting value and build trustworthiness.

Susan supports people who are challenged by these times and are trying to understand their role in and through this uncertainty. The pressures of AI, climate change, money chaos, political polarities and family needs can be overwhelming. She helps individuals deal with disorder and contradiction so they can act in empowered ways they find rewarding.

### How she works

Her coaching and planning work is selective, and it applies across diverse markets, industries and communities. With individuals and companies alike, the work re-examines leadership, service and product offers, business models, financial flows, competitor and collaborator relationships, and how performance is tracked and evaluated.

Each engagement acknowledges the complexities at hand and focuses on practical decisions in that context. Purpose is put into words, then made clear in roles, responsibilities, outcomes and adaptations, and sometimes in a new business plan or position.

Susan specializes in transforming concepts into products, founders into teams, and needs into shared opportunities. She brings joy and insight to tough and confusing times, knowing these complexities invite us to exercise our imagination as we build our future together.

### Background

Her early career included Time Inc.'s Washington bureau, followed by 25 years in telecom and information technology. She was recruited by Accel Partners and Seven Rosen to lead marketing for Aristacom, creators of the first commercial computer-telephony integration product. She served on the board of NATA, the North American Telecommunications Association, and has worked with new systems such as blockchain-managed services, gift economies, and peer-based saving and lending circles.

### Education

MBA, Saint Mary's College of California
