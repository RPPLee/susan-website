Tapestry

Story-making that builds and strengthens community

Tapestry is a group creative story-making and sharing experience focused on helping navigate today's uncertain, often scary, times and imagine new outcomes and ways to thrive. Over six months, a cohort of up to ten people travels a monthly, theme-based journey of observation, understanding, and learning with and about others.

Participants leave with creative written communications training — stories, plays, songs, poetry and more — and practical tools for more skillful listening and collaborating. As the course progresses, me, as one person, deepens into us as a multi-faceted entity.

  • Six months · twice monthly · two hours per meeting (24 hours total)
  • Cohorts of five to ten adults; mixed-age groups especially welcome
  • $400/person for a group of five · $300/person for six to ten

Turning Point Tenders

Navigating a significant life transition with six months of support and companionship

A tugboat tender guides a ship safely to its mooring. A business tender defines a relationship. A tender touch recognizes a wound. Turning Point Tenders is a private, curated six-month listening and coaching program for an individual at a moment when the past no longer exists and the future is not yet clear.

The engagement blends business management consulting, career and life coaching, spiritual exploration, and simple time spent with a trusted friend hashing out ideas. Every working session is a time of deep listening, grounded presence and reciprocal dialog.

  • Six months · ten hours per month (eight structured, two on-call)
  • Private one-to-one engagement with Susan Mills
  • Project framing, program design, monthly summaries, closing review
