**Your Turn, Next Move** shifts your question of *what’s next?* from pondering and dreaming into one or more action-based practical scenarios that enable you to explore a possible new life or business direction.

This structured suite of activities taps into your experience, insights, connections, fears, dreams and imagination as they affect a potential aspiration or important decision. At the conclusion, you will have articulated a concrete, high-level identification of one or more possibilities and ways to test them further.

Over two weeks you will

- Play games about serious stuff
- Search for patterns, themes, qualities, values and interests
- Express what you’d rather not say and invite in the hidden
- Identify the improbable, yet possible
- Talk, in meaningful conversation about deeply personal thoughts
- Outline what to do next

This package incorporates

- Five meetings (online) over two weeks
- Homework assignments after each meeting
- One written summary of the articulated choice(s)

**Your Turn, Next Move** lays the foundation for continued scenario development.

Follow-on tasks for business directions may take the form of formalized concepts and plans, product mockups, market tests, partnership explorations and such.

For personal directions, this work may grow into a guided and supported 90-day program of defined tasks to set and reach goals. It may lead to ongoing support for six-to-twelve months as a **Pivot Point Passage** assignment.
