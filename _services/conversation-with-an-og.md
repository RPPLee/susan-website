## What is Conversation With An OG?

Conversation With An OG is a free one-hour session of questions and dialog that aims to help people under 40, and others, navigate these complex times.

The exchange is rooted in deep listening and nonjudgmental holding of complex issues. Susan offers perspectives gained through eight decades of rich experiences, challenges and insights.

## Who Is It For?

- **People under 40** facing a decision, a transition or a question with no clean answer
- **Anyone** who wants an hour with someone who has seen a few cycles and will listen first
- **Founders, organizers and community leaders** working through something that does not fit a tidy category

## How It Works

Bring a question. Susan listens, asks, and offers what her experience suggests. There is no agenda, no report and nothing to buy. If a longer conversation would help, Susan will say so, and the next step is yours.

## What You'll Walk Away With

- A clearer view of the question you brought
- Perspectives you had not considered
- One or two practical next moves, if you want them
