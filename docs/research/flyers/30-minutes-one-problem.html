How it works

  1. Pick the problem. Something real and current: a decision you keep putting off, a person you can't get through to, a plan that has stalled.
  2. Book 30 minutes. Email or call, and we'll find a time. Phone or video, whichever you prefer.
  3. Work it through, live. Questions, then a way forward. You take the next step. If you want more, we can talk about that later.

Who it's for

  • Owners and managers of small businesses and nonprofits.
  • People stepping into a bigger role, or thinking about stepping out of one.
  • Anyone with a knot in their work they have been turning over alone.
  • Friends of friends. If someone handed you this, you're already invited.

One session per person. Not therapy, not legal or financial advice: a working conversation with someone who has seen a lot.

Susan Mills has spent eight decades listening, and most of them helping people run organizations: as an executive, a board member, a coach, and a mentor. She founded Metaphase Management Associates in Berkeley, California to keep doing what she likes best, which is sitting down with one person and one problem.
