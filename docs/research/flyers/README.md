Sample one-page flyer (US Letter) for a loosely described new offer, "30 Minutes, One Problem": a free 30-minute live session where Susan works through one real problem with one person, who then passes the flyer to friends. `30-minutes-one-problem.html` is the source; `30-minutes-one-problem.png` is a render of it. To re-render: `"/Applications/Google Chrome.app/Contents/MacOS/Google Chrome" --headless --disable-gpu --hide-scrollbars --force-device-scale-factor=1.5625 --window-size=816,1056 --screenshot=<png> file://<abs html path>`.

Every fact on the flyer is a placeholder (free, 30 minutes, phone or video, one session per person, who it is for, the bio wording, the QR target) until Susan confirms it.

Open questions for Susan: what actually happens in the 30 minutes; who it is for; free or paid, and if paid, how much; how people book (email, phone, a booking link); whether the session is recorded; and how many people she wants each person to pass it to.
