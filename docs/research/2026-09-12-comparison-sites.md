# Comparison consultant sites

Reviewed 2026-09-12. Susan sent these as sites she has been looking at. Feeds op-005 and op-022.

| Site | Platform | Has |
|---|---|---|
| unlockpowerwithin.org | Squarespace | 20+ client logos, four service categories, contact form, testimonials on service pages, no blog |
| chime-partners.com | WordPress (Divi) | named niche (nonprofit and K-12 leaders), 4-step process, four named testimonials, Calendly, curated Inspiration page instead of a blog |
| acrasyadvisors.com | Squarespace | sparse home page, Clients page, stale "coming 2024" promises |
| jodymichael.com | WordPress (agency) | video hero, book pushed twice, Fortune 500 logos, Trustpilot, mega-menu of about 15 items, heavy tracking. Susan does not like it: it reads as a sales funnel |

## What three or more have that metaphasemgt.com lacks

1. Named testimonials with title and organization.
2. A clients or "who I've worked with" section.
3. One repeated call to action with one verb.
4. A named audience in the hero.
5. Services grouped into three or four, with sub-offers underneath.
6. A content outlet: blog, resources page or newsletter.

None of the four publish prices. Metaphase does.

## Upgrades proposed (now op-022, approved)

Testimonials, audience line, three service groups (individuals, groups, organizations), one booking link, newsletter signup, fuller About page, an approach section, a share image.
