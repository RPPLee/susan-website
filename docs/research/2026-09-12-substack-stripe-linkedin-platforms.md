## Substack facts

- Subdomain change: Settings, Danger Zone. "Change subdomain without breaking links" can be used once, ever. Plain change breaks every link. https://support.substack.com/hc/en-us/articles/360037460112
- Publication name changes any time (Settings, Basics). Handle changes independently in account settings.
- Custom domain: $50 one-time, must be a subdomain such as `newsletter.metaphasemgt.com` since the apex serves the site. https://support.substack.com/hc/en-us/articles/360051222571
- Payments need the writer's own Stripe account. Minimums $5 a month, $30 a year. Substack takes 10%, Stripe 2.9% + 30 cents, plus 0.7% Stripe Billing on recurring. About 13 to 14% in total. https://support.substack.com/hc/en-us/articles/360037607131
- Substack sells subscriptions only. No one-off session, registration or ticket. https://support.substack.com/hc/en-us/articles/50773191186324
- Substack recommends a separate Stripe account from other business. Stripe allows several accounts under one login for one legal entity. https://docs.stripe.com/get-started/account/multiple-accounts
- As a website: theme colors, logo, wordmark, nav links, About page, sections. The homepage is always a post feed. No forms, booking or one-off checkout.

## Stripe for the site

- Payment Links: hosted checkout pages, no code, one-time or recurring, custom fields, promo codes, receipts. Standard card fee only. https://docs.stripe.com/payment-links
- Invoicing suits custom packages.

## LinkedIn

- Personal banner 1584 by 396, under 8 MB. https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/a568217
- Company page banner 1512 by 256, 3 MB. Logo 400 by 400. https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/a563309
- Featured section takes a link as a preview card. https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/a550399
- Custom button ("View my newsletter") needs Premium Business or above. https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/a1484520
- Free accounts list websites under Contact info.
- Only native Substack link: video posts auto-upload to LinkedIn.
